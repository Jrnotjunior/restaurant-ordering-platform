# Web2Table — BIR Regulatory Route Decision Brief

**Prepared:** 2026-10-20  
**Repository:** `Jrnotjunior/restaurant-ordering-platform` (`main`)  
**Purpose:** Decide what regulatory path the product is actually pursuing before designing invoice issuance, fiscal controls, reporting or BIR transmission.  
**Status:** Engineering decision brief based on current official BIR materials located on 20 October 2026. This is not legal/tax advice, not a BIR determination and not proof of accreditation.

## Decision in one sentence

Do not start implementing a purported “BIR-compliant invoice” or BIR API transmission until BIR / a Philippine tax professional confirms whether Web2Table should proceed as (1) a software supplier seeking Sales Machine/Software accreditation with tenant-user Permit to Use (PTU) handling, (2) an Electronic Invoicing Service Provider (ESP), or (3) both / a different approved integration model.

The two paths should not be conflated. They may have overlapping system requirements but differ in application route, role and approval.

### 1. RMO No. 24-2023: CRM/POS/other Sales Machine/Software path

Official BIR issuance index: https://www.bir.gov.ph/2023-Revenue-Memorandum-Order  
Official full text PDF: https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMO%20No.%2024-2023.pdf  
eAccReg official help: https://eaccreg.bir.gov.ph/ACCREG/help.html  
eAccReg enrollment form: https://eaccreg.bir.gov.ph/ACCREG/enrollment-create.html

The RMO covers accreditation of cash register machines, POS and other similar sales machines/software generating invoices/receipts, including subscription-based e-invoicing/e-receipting software. The official text includes these requirements relevant to product scope:

- For the traditional Sales Machine/Software path, the software must generate prescribed sales readings, including daily activity and adjustments such as refunds, returns, cancelled and void transactions, plus accumulated grand total sales. The Z-Reading must cover the entire sales operation/business day; once generated, additional transactions or adjustments for that same sales operation date must not be accepted. Readings must be reprintable for audit.
- The RMO explicitly says subscription-based e-invoicing/e-receipting software that **will transmit sales data to the BIR** does not need an electronic journal or equivalent, and does not need X-Reading, Z-Reading or equivalent. This is a conditional exception, not a blanket cloud-software exemption.
- Backend reports include a sales summary separating VATable, VAT-exempt and zero-rated sales, and other prescribed reports such as Senior Citizen, PWD, National Athletes and Coaches, Solo Parent and Void reports.
- The invoice/receipt serial generated by Sales Machine/Software must have a minimum six-digit running serial number. The existing `ORD-000001` sequence in Web2Table is the order number and cannot simply be reused or relabelled without the appropriate approved issuance model.
- The BIR eAccReg enrolment page separates a Supplier/Pseudo-Supplier profile from a Taxpayer profile and says enrollment data should be taken from the Certificate of Registration (BIR Form 2303).

These points imply that even if the conditional electronic-journal / X-Z exception applies, we should not infer that invoice numbering, backend reports, invoice field requirements, taxpayer data, application documents or approval are also waived.

### 2. eAccReg account and PTU route for software suppliers

The BIR's 2025 Citizens' Charter section for PTU CRM/POS/software states that taxpayers may apply online through Enhanced eAccReg, and that **only the Software Supplier/Provider account can apply for POS/CRM PTU on behalf of the taxpayer-user**; the taxpayer account can apply for SPM. The taxpayer-user prints the approved PTU; suppliers applying on the taxpayer-user's behalf can no longer print it.

Official reference: https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/BIR%20Citizen%27s%20Charter%20%282025-Edition%29.pdf

Action for this route: confirm current supplier/software accreditation prerequisites, eAccReg enrollment, accreditation/version identifiers, tenant-by-tenant PTU process and any required change notification/update for software releases. Treat each tenant's PTU/registration state as separate data from platform-wide supplier accreditation.

### 3. RR No. 11-2025, amended by RR No. 26-2025: electronic invoice scope and transition period

Official RR No. 11-2025: https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%2011-2025.pdf  
Official RR No. 26-2025 full text: https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2026-2025.pdf  
Official RR No. 26-2025 digest: https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2026-2025%20Digest.pdf

The rules define structured electronic invoices capable of electronic extraction and transmission to the BIR for sales reporting. RR No. 26-2025 extended the compliance period to **31 December 2026** for the listed taxpayer groups, including:
- Small, medium and large taxpayers engaged in e-commerce/internet transactions (micro taxpayers are excluded from this stated group);
- taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Service;
- taxpayers classified as large under the applicable EOPT rules; and
- taxpayers using CAS, CBA with accounting records and other invoicing software.

RR No. 11-2025 also lists POS users among groups whose electronic-invoice mandate is to apply once the BIR establishes a system capable of storing and processing the required data, with requirements to be prescribed separately. The rules distinguish printing a software-generated invoice on paper from a true electronic invoice: paper output without required electronic reporting capability does not, by itself, qualify as an electronic invoice.

**Implication:** tenant applicability must be determined from the tenant's tax classification, activities, systems and currently effective BIR implementation instructions. We must not claim either that all Web2Table tenants share a single deadline or that the 31 December 2026 extension removes supplier-accreditation obligations.

### 4. RMC No. 98-2026: current electronic invoice guidance

The official BIR 2026 RMC index lists RMC No. 98-2026, issued 22 September 2026, with the subject: “Prescribing Policies and Guidelines on the Issuance of Electronic Invoice under Revenue Regulations (RR) No. 8-2022 and RR No. 11-2025, as Amended by RR No. 26-2025.”

Official index: https://www.bir.gov.ph/2026-Revenue-Memorandum-Circulars  
BIR EIS public portal and advisories: https://eis.bir.gov.ph/

The full text and any incorporated technical annexes must be obtained and clause-mapped before development. This brief does not assume a specific payload schema, API authentication method, transmission timing, acknowledgement format or certification test from the issuance title alone.

### 5. RR No. 6-2026: invoice mandatory information

The official 2026 Revenue Regulations index lists RR No. 6-2026, dated 1 October 2026, as amending mandatory invoice information under Section 6(B) of RR No. 7-2024 to include creditable withholding-tax information.

Official index: https://www.bir.gov.ph/2026-Revenue-Regulations

This is another reason not to implement a static invoice template from a 2024 sample. The complete text must be mapped to transaction-specific conditional fields with a tax professional; do not indiscriminately add withholding-tax amounts to all restaurant invoices or omit them where applicable.

A window being open does not mean Web2Table is eligible or that the ESP route is the correct route for its desired product.

## Route comparison

| Question | Route A — Software supplier / Sales Machine-Software accreditation | Route B — ESP / structured electronic invoice and sales-data transmission |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Accreditation and permitted use of Sales Machine/Software; supplier applies for relevant PTUs for taxpayer-users where applicable | BIR-recognized service-provider route for electronic-invoice/electronic-sales-reporting integration, subject to current ESP criteria |
| Core authority to start from | RMO No. 24-2023 and current eAccReg / PTU instructions | RR Nos. 8-2022, 11-2025 and 26-2025; RMC No. 98-2026; current ESP application materials |
| Tenant-level handling | Confirm supplier enrollment/accreditation and PTU/registration path separately for each tenant-user | Confirm the tenant's applicability, registration/onboarding, authorization and responsibility split with BIR |
| Serial/issue ledger | Prescribed six-digit minimum running serial under RMO 24-2023 is a key requirement for Sales Machine/Software; validate exact approved allocation/format | Use the format, identifiers and issue rules specified by the current electronic-invoice guidance and approved technical specification; do not presume order numbers equal invoice numbers |
| Journal / X-Z readings | Required as applicable under RMO; the stated subscription-based system exception applies only if it will transmit sales data to BIR | The transmission-based exception may be relevant only if the exact system/contract and actual implementation qualify; obtain confirmation |
| Backend reports | RMO requires relevant sales/adjustment/discount and backend reporting capabilities | Determine the structured reporting duties under the current EIS/e-invoice route; do not assume traditional reports disappear unless authority says so |
| Deployment / certification | Track approved software/version identifiers, hardware or deployment model, supplier status, taxpayer-user PTU and release governance | Track ESP application/approval, technical certification, API/interface, keys, payload schema, acknowledgement/retry rules, support and tenant onboarding |
| Status today | Route is available in BIR's established eAccReg process, but Web2Table has no approval evidenced in this audit | An ESP application window is currently advertised for 16–30 October 2026; BIR publicly warned on 14 September it had not yet accredited any ESP |

## Required written questions for BIR / Philippine tax counsel

The decision owner should request written answers, using a concise architecture diagram and product description. The questions are:

1. For a multi-tenant SaaS that supports restaurant web ordering and cashier POS, which parts of Web2Table are classified as Sales Machine/Software, invoicing software, electronic invoicing/receipting service, or ESP integration?
2. Must Web2Table apply for Sales Machine/Software supplier accreditation and handle individual tenant-user PTUs, ESP accreditation, or both? Is there a specific approved hybrid path?
3. Does the RMO No. 24-2023 subscription-based e-invoicing/e-receipting exception apply to our proposed design only if Web2Table itself transmits sales data to BIR? What exact registration, transmission, test/certification and evidence is necessary for that exception?
4. What exact current invoice structure/mandatory data applies after RR No. 6-2026, RR No. 7-2024 as amended, and RMC No. 98-2026, including buyer/withholding and conditional fields?
5. Which tenant categories must issue electronic invoices by 31 December 2026 under RR No. 26-2025, how is a small/medium/large/micro taxpayer classified for this purpose, and which POS-user requirements are already operational versus awaiting separate BIR implementation?
6. What identifiers/serial rules apply to issued invoices, what is the correct way to handle sequence continuity, voided/unused numbers, duplicate/reprints, offline operation, refunds and corrections?
7. Which reports are mandatory on our chosen route (sales summary, VATable/VAT-exempt/zero-rated, Senior Citizen/PWD/Solo Parent/etc., voids/adjustments, daily readings, accumulated grand totals), and which exact report annex/schema should acceptance tests implement?
8. What is the correct current application/technical certification path, and what supporting documents, software version identifiers, technical test environment, API specifications, data-security requirements, and service-level/failure-recovery behavior are required?
9. Under the current ESP application window (16–30 October 2026), is Web2Table eligible to submit and what is the exact application checklist and cutoff time?
10. What system/version changes require notice, reassessment, re-certification, a new PTU, or additional approvals?

## Work that can safely proceed before the route decision

These engineering tasks do not require choosing the final BIR transmission route and can be prepared on a feature branch with tests, without changing production data:

1. **Data protection inventory:** list every code path that writes to `orders` directly and every function that can change amount, payment state, order number or discounts. Mark legitimate operations (such as dispatch/status transitions) and propose narrow RPC boundaries. Do not apply a blanket revoke.
2. **Financial fixture specification:** collect accountant-approved examples for VAT/non-VAT, inclusive/exclusive prices, statutory discounts, loyalty/promotion discounts, delivery fees, payment channels, rounding, refunds and voids. No production formula changes until expected outputs are signed off.
3. **Reporting consistency tests:** reproduce the known loyalty-discount double-count risk in a deterministic test using fixtures. Keep current reports labelled as support data until reconciled.
4. **Invoice readiness model draft (not production migration):** design fields for tenant taxpayer profile, branch identity, registration/approval status, software version, tax calculation state/version and separately numbered invoice records. Wait on exact required fields/serial semantics and approvals before applying schema.
5. **Evidence package skeleton:** version-controlled checklist of each official clause, test case, output evidence and reviewer/approval status.

## Do not do these things yet

- Do not print “BIR-approved,” “BIR-accredited” or “official invoice” in Web2Table UI, marketing or documents.
- Do not route arbitrary client-supplied invoice data directly to a BIR endpoint.
- Do not label the existing order number as an invoice serial.
- Do not make up API payloads, certificate types, transmission deadlines or retry rules from assumptions.
- Do not automatically backfill tax snapshots or issue invoices against legacy orders.
- Do not alter production table privileges broadly without mapping legitimate UI writes and having a rollback plan.
- Do not treat the existence of an ESP window or an eAccReg page as approval or qualification.

## Immediate next action

Within the current October 16–30 ESP application window, obtain and review the full BIR ESP advisory/checklist and RMC No. 98-2026 full text/annexes; have the product owner decide whether to pursue Route A, Route B, or both based on written BIR/counsel guidance. In parallel, create a **non-production** branch with the direct-order-update inventory and report reconciliation tests. Only after these items are complete should we implement database-hardening and unified sale-finalization changes.
