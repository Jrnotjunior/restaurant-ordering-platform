## Draft letter

Dear Sir/Madam,

We respectfully request written guidance on the applicable BIR registration, accreditation, and authorization requirements for **Web2Table**, a multi-tenant cloud-based restaurant ordering and management platform being developed for use by separate restaurant businesses in the Philippines.

The intended product includes customer-facing restaurant websites and online ordering, a cashier-facing point-of-sale workflow, dine-in/pickup/take-out/delivery order management, payment integrations, operational sales reports, and printed customer receipts. Each restaurant tenant maintains its own restaurant identity and ordering domain. The platform is designed as a shared SaaS service rather than a separate single-taxpayer system installed exclusively for one taxpayer. Current checkout and POS receipts are operational outputs; we are not claiming BIR accreditation or authority to issue official invoices.

Before designing an invoice-issuance or BIR-transmission implementation, we would appreciate confirmation of the correct regulatory route and the scope of any required software accreditation, taxpayer-level registration, permit, certification, or transmission obligations.

Specifically, please clarify the following:

1. **Classification and applicant role.** Should Web2Table apply as a supplier/provider of POS or other Sales Machine/Software under RMO No. 24-2023, as an Electronic Invoicing Service Provider (ESP) under the applicable e-invoicing rules, as both, or under another authorized model? Does providing a shared SaaS platform to separate restaurant taxpayers change the classification?

2. **Tenant-specific requirements.** For the selected route, what must Web2Table obtain as the software provider, and what must each restaurant taxpayer separately obtain? Please distinguish supplier/software accreditation, Permit to Use (PTU) or other system registration, Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice, EIS certification, Permit to Transmit (PTT), taxpayer onboarding, and any branch- or version-specific approval as applicable.

3. **Subscription-based software exception.** Under RMO No. 24-2023, what precise conditions apply to the exception for subscription-based electronic invoicing/receipting software that transmits sales data to the BIR? What registration, transmission capability, testing, certification and evidence must exist for Web2Table to qualify, and which obligations remain even if the exception applies?

4. **Current electronic invoicing rules and data fields.** How should the planned solution apply RR No. 7-2024 as amended, RR Nos. 11-2025 and 26-2025, RMC No. 98-2026, RR No. 6-2026 on mandatory invoice information including creditable withholding-tax information, and any later effective issuances or technical specifications? Please identify the current official invoice schema, taxpayer/customer fields, identifiers, sequence rules, document status/adjustment rules, and required report data relevant to restaurant sales.

5. **Electronic invoicing / ESP application window.** The BIR website advertises an ESP application window from 16 to 30 October 2026. Please confirm the current official application advisory, applicable applicant qualification criteria, documentary checklist, technical/security and data-governance requirements, testing stages, and whether this route is intended for a new multi-tenant SaaS supplier such as Web2Table. Please provide the official full text and correct issuance number of any subsequent circulars governing the current ESP application, so we can avoid relying on unverified or inconsistent secondary copies.

6. **Readings and reports.** For the confirmed route, which prescribed invoice/receipt serials, X-Reading/Z-Reading or equivalent readings, accumulated grand totals, electronic/audit journals, sales/discount/void/refund reports, and daily close controls are required? If the subscription-based transmission exception applies, please specify exactly which of these controls are waived and which remain mandatory.

7. **System testing and change control.** What demonstration, testing, conformance, audit, data retention, backup/recovery, version approval, material-change notification, incident reporting and ongoing monitoring evidence is required before live taxpayer transactions can use Web2Table?

8. **Permitted development and test environment.** May Web2Table conduct demonstrations and controlled pre-production tests before approval? What safeguards and labels are required to ensure test transactions are not presented as authorized invoices or treated as live taxpayer transactions?

We can provide an architecture overview, example operational receipt, multi-tenant data-flow diagram, draft test plan and the intended division of responsibilities between Web2Table and each restaurant taxpayer. We will not label outputs as BIR-accredited or official invoices, or enable production electronic-invoice transmission, until the applicable requirements and approvals have been confirmed and obtained.

Thank you for your guidance. We request a written response identifying the relevant BIR issuance(s), responsible office, applicable application route, and next procedural steps.
